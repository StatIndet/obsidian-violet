# Third-party assets

The MacTahoe GTK theme's 2× PNG title-button assets are embedded as data URLs
in `src/_titlebutton-assets.scss` and the generated `theme.css`.

- Source: <https://github.com/vinceliuice/MacTahoe-gtk-theme>
- Local source revision: `f19899811eff6d127afc38c4fa4981b220cb2ea2`
- Original path: `src/assets/gtk/windows-assets/titlebutton/`
- License: MIT; full notice in `licenses/MACTAHOE.txt`

The controls remain Obsidian's built-in buttons. Only their appearance and
placement are changed.
